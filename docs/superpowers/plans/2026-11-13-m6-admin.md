# M6 Admin and Billing Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Members limited to document types (ADM-1), plans with quotas and a usage meter (ADM-3), annual MAD invoices paid by bank transfer (ADM-4), and a self-serve full export with account closure after a 30-day grace period (ADM-5). Backend only: no screens in M6.

**Architecture:**
- **Document-type grants.** They sit on `Membership` in the control-plane DB. `WorkspaceContext` carries the user's effective set of types. Each read path filters on the type in the workspace's index DB (`search_doc.document_type`). Ask stores the asker's set with the question, so `worker-ask` filters too.
- **Billing tables.** Billing is per organisation, in the control-plane DB of the org's region: `Plan` (seeded), `Subscription`, `Invoice`, `UsageRecord`.
  - The meter is computed from the ledger and memberships, plus `UsageRecord` rows for AI pages that the extraction worker writes.
  - One function, `sijil_core.billing.standing(org)`, says what the org may do. Every gate (extraction, new member, export, write) asks it.
- **Invoices.** They are issued in Python `Decimal` and numbered gaplessly per year.
  - Each is rendered once to a fr/ar PDF (reportlab with arabic-reshaper and python-bidi, already used by `fixtures/generate.py`).
  - The PDF bytes and their SHA-256 are stored on the invoice row, so the document never changes.
  - Platform staff mark a transfer as paid through a staff-only API.
- **Closure.** The Owner requests it with step-up; a full export ("all" periods) is queued, and a 30-day grace period starts that the Owner can cancel. After the grace period there are two cases:
  - No document is still under retention: the tenant is destroyed.
  - Some still are: the tenant is hibernated. Its Paperless is stopped, and the Owner has read-only access served from our index DB and our object store until retention ends. A signed take-over statement ends the hosting earlier, but never deletes a locked original before its date.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, SQLAlchemy 2 async, Alembic (control-plane DB), Celery beat, reportlab, arabic-reshaper, python-bidi, num2words (the total in words, fr and ar).

**Spec:**
- `SPEC.md`: ADM-1, ADM-3, ADM-4 and ADM-5. Control-plane entities `Plan`, `Subscription`, `Invoice` and `UsageRecord` ("standard billing fields").

**Product-owner decisions for M6 (2026-11-13):**
- **Catalogue.** Annual prices in MAD, excluding VAT. Users and storage are current totals; documents and AI pages are counted per billing year. Values are seeded and editable later by migration.

  | Plan code | Price (MAD, excl. VAT) | Users | Documents/year | Storage | AI pages/year |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `essentiel` | 2 400.00 | 3 | 2 000 | 5 GB | 2 000 |
  | `pro` | 6 000.00 | 10 | 10 000 | 25 GB | 10 000 |
  | `entreprise` | 14 400.00 | 30 | 40 000 | 100 GB | 40 000 |

  VAT is 20%. There is no free plan. A new org starts a 30-day trial with the `essentiel` quotas.
- **Terms.** An invoice is due 30 days after issue. Once it is overdue, AI extraction, new users and exports stop. 30 days after the due date, the workspace becomes read-only plus capture. Payment lifts everything at once.
- **Mark paid.** A staff-only API (`/v1/staff/...`) behind a platform-staff flag. Staff are bootstrapped by a CLI command.
- **Seller details** are real. The product owner supplies them, and they live in config (`SIJIL_SELLER_*`), never in code.

## Global Constraints

- **Paperless-ngx stays unmodified.** It is reached only through `sijil_core.paperless`. Hibernation stops and starts the tenant's containers through `TenantRuntime`; it never changes the image.
- **No JS package talks to Paperless.** M6 adds no JS code.
- **Region.**
  - Every workspace-scoped and org-scoped request and job calls `assert_region`. This includes staff routes, which act only on orgs of their own region.
  - Billing data stays in the region's control-plane DB.
- **Retention lock (absolute).**
  - Closure, hibernation, take-over and destruction never delete or change a document under retention, and never shorten an object lock. Destruction purges an original only through the existing removal-key path, and only once its `retention_until` has passed.
  - Billing restrictions never open a delete or alter path.
  - Every new path that can change or delete a document gets a cell in `tests/retention/test_lock_all_paths.py`.
- **Capture and read are never blocked** (§5.7a), neither by quota, overdue, read-only nor the closure grace period:
  - upload, inbound email and ingestion keep working;
  - reading the list, search, document views and downloads keeps working.

  The only exception is a hibernated workspace, which has no capture because its tenant is stopped and the org is closing.
- **Integrity and audit.**
  - Every billing, closure and grant change is audited through the existing hash-chained append (`sijil_core.audit` / `sijil_cp.org_audit`).
  - Audit `data` carries codes, ids, counts and amounts as strings, never prose.
  - Never write an UPDATE or DELETE against `audit_event`.
- **Money is `Decimal`** in Python, `numeric(14,2)` in SQL and a string in JSON. Rounding is `ROUND_HALF_UP` to 0.01. No float anywhere, PDF included.
- **Machine codes, never prose.**
  - New API codes:
    - `members.types_invalid` 422;
    - `members.types_not_allowed_for_role` 422;
    - `org.ice_invalid` 422;
    - `org.ice_required` 422;
    - `billing.plan_unknown` 422;
    - `billing.overdue` 402;
    - `billing.read_only` 402;
    - `quota.users_exceeded` 409;
    - `invoice.not_found` 404;
    - `invoice.already_paid` 409;
    - `staff.forbidden` 404 (no existence leak);
    - `closure.already_requested` 409;
    - `closure.not_requested` 404;
    - `closure.grace_over` 409;
    - `takeover.invalid` 422.
  - Invoice PDF labels come from `packages/i18n/messages/{fr,ar}.json` (new `invoice.*` keys, added in en too). Quota warnings are codes: `quota.warning_80` and `quota.warning_100`.
- **Shared code** goes in `sijil_core`. Never import `sijil_cp` from the worker, or the reverse.
- **Tooling.** Python 3.13, ruff (line length 100), `mypy --strict`, pytest `asyncio_mode=auto`. TDD: each test is seen failing first. Conventional Commits, kept small. SAMPLE fixtures only.
- **Migrations.**
  - Control plane: Alembic from `0013` upward, one migration per task that needs one.
  - Index DB: `SCHEMA_VERSION` 8 → 9 (Task 1, `ask_query.document_types`) through `ensure_schema`.
- **Integration tests** create their own org and append it to `created_workspaces`. The teardown now also stops the tenant's containers (Task 6), fixing the leak that ran Ollama out of memory in the M5 acceptance run.

## Review Focus

1. **Type leak through a side door.** A Member limited to invoices must never learn of a payslip by any route:
   - the document list, a document by id (404, not 403);
   - its original file, the review queue, search hits and counts;
   - saved views, tags, Ask figures, citations and retrieval;
   - an export (403: Members cannot export);
   - an unclassified document they did not upload themselves.
2. **Gapless numbering under concurrency.** Two invoices issued at the same moment, such as a renewal beat and a plan upgrade, never share a number and never leave a gap. A rolled-back issue gives its number back.
3. **The money edges.**
   - Proration for an upgrade on the last day, and on the first day.
   - VAT rounding on .005 values; TTC is HT plus VAT exactly.
   - The total in words in Arabic matches the digits.
   - A change of plan price never alters an issued invoice.
4. **Capture survives every billing state.** Over every quota, overdue, read-only, and in the grace period, upload and inbound email still land and are ingested. Read-only refuses review, seal, tags, fields, retention changes and Ask, with `billing.read_only`.
5. **Closure against the lock.** A take-over statement, the destruction job or a cancelled-then-requested closure never deletes an original before its `retention_until`. Hibernated access stays Owner-only and read-only, and survives the Paperless containers being stopped.

- Ask: the asker's set is stored on `ask_query.document_types` (`text[]`, NULL for all). `worker-ask` applies it to the figures, the needs-review exclusion count, retrieval and citations. An intent naming a type outside the set gets the same answer as "no matching documents"; it never says the documents exist.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `Plan(code, price_ht: Decimal, max_users, max_documents_year, max_storage_bytes, max_ai_pages_year, active)`;
  - `Subscription(org_id unique, plan_code, status, trial_ends_at, period_start, period_end, pending_plan_code)`, where status is `trial | active | overdue | read_only | closed`;
  - `UsageRecord(org_id, workspace_id, ledger_id, kind='ai_pages', quantity, at)`, unique on `(ledger_id, kind)`;
  - `billing.meter(db, org_id, now) -> Meter` with per quota `used`, `limit`, `percent` and `level` (`ok | warn_80 | over_100`);
  - `billing.standing(db, org_id, now) -> Standing` with `ai_allowed`, `new_users_allowed`, `export_allowed`, `writes_allowed` and `reason_code`;
  - `GET /v1/orgs/{id}/usage`, `GET /v1/orgs/{id}/subscription`, `PUT /v1/orgs/{id}/subscription {plan_code}`. The PUT is Owner-only with step-up; the invoice it issues comes in Task 3.

**Rules:**
- A new org gets a `trial` subscription on `essentiel` with `trial_ends_at` 30 days after creation. The billing year is `[period_start, period_end)`, starting at the end of the trial.
- **Meter:**
  - users: the distinct users with a membership in the org;
  - documents: ledger rows ingested in the billing year (or the trial), excluding rejected duplicates;
  - storage: the sum of `size_bytes` of originals not yet purged (`original_removed_at IS NULL`);
  - AI pages: the sum of `UsageRecord.quantity` in the billing year.
- **AI pages** are written by the extraction worker once per document, idempotently, when it calls the model. The quantity is the page count Paperless reports for the document, at least 1. Before calling the model, the worker asks `standing`. If AI isn't allowed, the document goes to review with no suggestions, flag `quota_exceeded` (or `billing_overdue`), and no model call and no usage record.
- **Warnings:** the first time a quota reaches 80% or 100% in a billing year, `quota.warning_80` / `quota.warning_100` `{kind}` is audited, and the usage API reports the level.
- **Over 100% of any quota:** AI stops and new members get 409 `quota.users_exceeded`. Capture and reads continue (Global Constraints).
- **Organisation ICE:** `PATCH /orgs/{id}` accepts `ice`, 15 digits (`org.ice_invalid`). Choosing a plan requires it (`org.ice_required`). Once set, the own-ICE guard of M5 uses it first: the org's ICE is never learnt, never hinted, and is flagged `supplier_own_ice`. The frequency detection stays as the fallback for orgs without an ICE.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `Invoice(id, org_id, number, year, kind, plan_code, period_start, period_end, amount_ht, vat_rate, amount_vat, amount_ttc, currency, issued_at, due_at, paid_at, payment_reference, state, buyer jsonb, seller jsonb, locale, pdf bytea, pdf_sha256)`, where kind is `annual | upgrade` and state is `issued | overdue | paid`;
  - `invoicing.amounts(price_ht: Decimal, fraction: Fraction | None) -> Amounts`;
  - `invoicing.issue(db, org, kind, plan, period, now) -> Invoice`;
  - `invoice_pdf.render(invoice, messages) -> bytes`;
  - `GET /v1/orgs/{id}/invoices`, `GET /v1/orgs/{id}/invoices/{inv}`, `GET /v1/orgs/{id}/invoices/{inv}/pdf` (Owner and Admin).

**Rules:**
- **Number:** `SIJ-{YYYY}-{NNNNNN}`, gapless per calendar year of issue. It comes from `InvoiceCounter(year)` locked `FOR UPDATE` in the issuing transaction, so a rollback returns the number.
- **Amounts:** `vat = round(ht × 0.20)`, `ttc = ht + vat`, `ROUND_HALF_UP` to 0.01.
  - An upgrade mid-year charges the difference for the days left: `(new − old) × days_left / days_in_period`, then VAT.
  - A downgrade is stored as `pending_plan_code` and applies at renewal, with no credit note.
- **Snapshots:** the buyer (org name, ICE, address if any) and the seller (from config) are copied into the invoice row at issue. Later changes to the plan, seller or org never alter an issued invoice.
- **Seller config:** `SIJIL_SELLER_NAME`, `_ADDRESS`, `_ICE`, `_IF`, `_RC`, `_TP` (taxe professionnelle), `_RIB`, `_BANK`, all required, with no defaults in code. `.env.example` carries SAMPLE values; the real ones go in the deployment env.
- **PDF:** A4, one page.
  - It carries the fr or ar labels per the owner's locale (`ar` is RTL, reshaped and bidi-ordered as in `fixtures/generate.py`).
  - It shows seller and buyer with their ICE, the number, issue and due dates, the plan and period, HT, VAT 20% and TTC, the total in words (`num2words`, `lang='fr'` or `'ar'`), and the RIB and transfer reference to quote (the invoice number).
  - It is rendered once at issue, with its SHA-256 stored. The bytes are deterministic: no timestamps in the PDF metadata (`invariant=True`).
- **`sijil.billing_daily`** (beat, once a day, in this region):
  - a trial that has ended moves to `active` and issues the first annual invoice. Without an ICE, the subscription becomes `overdue` with reason `org.ice_required` and no invoice;
  - a renewal invoice is issued 30 days before `period_end`, applying `pending_plan_code`;
  - an invoice past `due_at` and unpaid moves to `overdue`, and the subscription to `overdue`;
  - 30 days after `due_at`, the subscription moves to `read_only`.
  - Each transition is audited (`billing.invoice_issued`, `billing.overdue`, `billing.read_only`).
- **Gates:** `standing` maps `overdue` to no AI, no new users and no exports (`billing.overdue` 402 on `POST /exports`). It maps `read_only` to the same, plus every state-changing workspace route except upload returning `billing.read_only` 402: review, fields, seal, tags, corrections, retention, saved views and Ask.
  - Reads, downloads and capture are never gated (Review Focus 4).
  - Closure stays allowed in every state (Task 5).

  - `POST /v1/workspaces/{id}/exports {"period": "all"}`: every non-terminal document, reusing the 4.6 ZIP, index and signed integrity report;
  - `POST /v1/orgs/{id}/closure`, `DELETE /v1/orgs/{id}/closure` (cancel) and `GET /v1/orgs/{id}/closure`, Owner-only, POST and DELETE with step-up;
  - `POST /v1/orgs/{id}/closure/takeover` (multipart: the signed statement PDF plus the signer's name and capacity), Owner-only with step-up;
  - `TenantRuntime.stop(ref)` and `TenantRuntime.start(ref)`.

**Rules:**
- **Request:**
  - `Closure(org_id, requested_at, requested_by, grace_ends_at = requested_at + 30 days, export_job_id, state)`, where state is `grace | hibernated | destroyed | cancelled`.
  - The org status becomes `closing`. A full export is queued for each workspace of the org, and the request is audited `closure.requested`.
  - During the grace period the workspace keeps working, under the billing gates as they stand. No renewal invoice is issued.
- **Cancel** (only during the grace period) restores org status `active` and audits `closure.cancelled`. A second request after a cancel starts a new grace period.
- **`sijil.closure_daily`**, for each closure whose grace period has ended:
  - If no document of the workspace has `retention_until` on or after today (region-local) and none is sealed under a compliance lock still in force, the workspace is `destroyed`:
    1. `TenantRuntime.destroy`;
    2. the index DB is dropped;
    3. originals are purged through the removal worker;
    4. the ledger rows stay as tombstones;
    5. it is audited `closure.destroyed`.
  - Otherwise the workspace is `hibernated`:
    1. `TenantRuntime.stop`;
    2. workspace state `hibernated`;
    3. the subscription `closed`;
    4. it is audited `closure.hibernated`.
- **Hibernated access:** only Owners can read. They get the document list, search, document metadata, originals streamed from our object store, the audit log and exports. Paperless is never called. Every other role gets 404. Every write returns `billing.read_only`. Capture is refused (`ingest.workspace_not_active` already exists).
- **Retention end:** while hibernated, `closure_daily` runs the existing deletion-eligibility path as documents pass `retention_until`. When none is left under retention, the workspace is destroyed as above. The Owner's approval for those deletions is the closure request itself, recorded in the audit as `closure.deletion_approved`.
- **Take-over statement** (design §7 item 11, pending legal confirmation): the Owner uploads a signed statement saying the org takes custody of its originals, and has downloaded the full export (the export must be `ready`; otherwise `takeover.invalid`).
  - The statement is stored write-once with its SHA-256, and audited `closure.takeover_signed`.
  - After the grace period it ends Sijil's hosting: tenant destroyed, index dropped, Owner access ends.
  - Originals still under retention are **not deleted early**. Their object locks stand, and the removal worker purges each one when its `retention_until` passes, with no further approval. This keeps the absolute lock (Global Constraints) and reads §7 item 11 conservatively. Ruling for the product owner and legal.
